Does Andi replace Ampersand?

No. Andi extends Ampersand by building connectors for the platform. Ampersand remains the infrastructure you use to configure, operate, and monitor your integrations.

Can Andi build a connector for any system?

Bring the request. Andi scopes what is feasible with you. Provider APIs, permissions, available access, and the operations your product needs determine what can be built. The catalog is a starting point, not a promise of universal API coverage.

What if we don’t have our own sandbox?

Share a secure link with your customer so they can provide sandbox credentials directly. Andi uses that access for read-only validation, so you can avoid waiting through a separate vendor sandbox process.
